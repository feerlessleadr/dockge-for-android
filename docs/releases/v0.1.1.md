# Dockge for Android 0.1.1

A design update. Nothing about how the app works with your servers has changed.

## What is new
- **New look, still Material 3.** Large titles, tonal cards, status tiles and chips, a bottom bar with Instances, Alerts and Settings.
- **Stacks.** Search plus filters (All, Running, Updates, Stopped). Update is the one filled button on a stack, with an update card showing the image digests.
- **Update progress.** The same fixed-size sheet, now a checklist with a progress bar.
- **Two app icons.** Choose Blue or Light in Settings. Both support Android 13 themed icons.

## Good to know
- If you added the app to your home screen from 0.1.0, that shortcut may disappear after this update (the launcher entry changed). Add it again from the app drawer. Switching icons does the same.
- Requirements and known limits are unchanged from 0.1.0.

## Install
Obtainium updates from the 0.1.0 install. By hand: download the APK below and compare its SHA-256 with the value at the end of these notes. Same signing key as 0.1.0.
